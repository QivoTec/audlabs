Privacy Policy.

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Welcome to AudLabs. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our platform at audlabs.io and any related services.

Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you:

  • Sign in using Google Authentication
  • Purchase credits via bank transfer, cryptocurrency or card payment
  • Generate voiceovers or clone voices on our platform
  • Contact us for support
  • Participate in our Affiliate Program

The types of information we collect include:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address and profile photo from Google
  • Payment Information: Bank account details (for withdrawals), transaction history and payment records
  • Usage Data: Voiceovers generated, voices cloned, credits used and generation history
  • Device Information: Browser type, IP address and device identifiers

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your AudLabs account
  • Process your credit purchases and withdrawals
  • Generate voiceovers and process voice cloning requests
  • Send you welcome emails and important account notifications
  • Calculate and pay affiliate commissions
  • Improve our platform and develop new features
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform security
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Payment Processing

AudLabs uses the following third-party payment processors:

  • Paystack — for Nigerian Naira bank transfers and instant withdrawals
  • Flutterwave — for card payments and mobile money across Africa and internationally
  • NOWPayments — for cryptocurrency (USDT) payments

These processors have their own privacy policies and handle payment data according to their respective security standards. We do not store your full card details on our servers.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase Firestore, which is hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Your voiceover generation history is stored locally on your device and in our secure database to enable cross-device access.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party partners who help us deliver our services including payment processing and voice generation
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our legal rights
  • Business Transfer: In the event of a merger or acquisition of AudLabs

6. Affiliate Program

When you participate in our Affiliate Program, we track affiliates using a unique affiliate code linked to your account. We record commission earnings and payments in your account. Affiliate earnings are calculated at 10% of every payment made by users you refer.

7. Voice Data

When you use our voice cloning feature, audio files you upload are processed by our voice generation systems to create your custom voice. These audio files are used solely for the purpose of creating the voice clone you requested.

We do not use your voice samples for any purpose other than creating the voice clone you requested.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies and local storage to:

  • Keep you signed in to your account
  • Remember your theme preference (dark or light mode)
  • Store your voiceover generation history locally
  • Track affiliate codes from affiliate links

We use only essential cookies necessary for the platform to function.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@audlabs.io

10. Children's Privacy

AudLabs is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately at hello@audlabs.io.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by sending an email to your registered email address or by posting a notice on our platform. Your continued use of AudLabs after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
